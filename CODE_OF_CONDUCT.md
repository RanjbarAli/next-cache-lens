# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, and welcome people of every background and experience level.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information, sustained disruption, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Reports should use a private maintainer channel when possible. Do not disclose another person's private information in a public issue.

Maintainers will evaluate reports fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and apply consequences proportionately.
